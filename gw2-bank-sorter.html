For Guild Wars 2

Bank Sorter

Reads your bank through ArenaNet's official API, then tells you what to deposit, use, sell or salvage and how to lay out your tabs. It only reads your account. You move the items yourself in game.

Create one at account.arena.net/applications with account and inventories ticked. Tick characters to include every character's bags, unlocks to check which dyes, minis, recipes and skins you already own, and progression to protect items your collections still need. The key is sent only to api.guildwars2.com.

Bank and bags

Suggested bank layout once the plan below is done. Every category gets its own tab when you have room; smaller groups share. Split stacks are shown merged.

Cleanup plan

Work top to bottom and tick items off as you go. Ticks are saved in this browser. The API updates your bank and bags a few minutes after you change them in game, so press Refresh once you're done.

Still checking your collections. Hold off on selling, salvaging or destroying anything until this finishes. Items your collections still need will drop out of the plan.
